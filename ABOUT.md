# Saad Chinoy — Make Things That Make Sense

*Singapore · Maker-at-Large*

**The Caffeine-Powered Creative. Technologist for the Common Good.**

I'm Saad Chinoy — a maker, systems designer, open-hardware advocate, and biomaker working at the intersection of art, science, and community. For what feels like a lifetime, I've been building tools, spaces, and practices that put technology in service of people — not the other way around.

## About

Equal parts barista, biologist, and systems thinker. SalvageGarden began in 2018 as a volunteer interest group I co-founded with a number of other volunteers, as a space where broken things become building blocks — where e-waste becomes educational kit and discarded hardware becomes assistive technology. It remained an interest group until it was formalised in 2022 with the help of LJ (Leong Chia Jang).

EdibleMakerspace is where that same salvage instinct meets food and citizen science — a DIY bio community space for fermentation, environmental sensing, and participatory design. Its current work includes CitizenQuack: sensor nodes inside 3D-printed ducks (or whatever creature the community chooses that month) whose posture, colour and quack change with what they are sensing — air quality, humidity, noise, and more — built from the FAB25 "Smart Citizen Meets Meshtastic" hackathon, and now feeding a Singapore node of PLANETAI.

My practice lives at the art-science boundary: co-creating tactile games with disability communities, fermenting SCOBY bioplastics, running microbial fuel cell workshops, and building open-source braille printers across Southeast Asia.

I believe open source is infrastructure for democracy. The recipe should always be shared.

### Guiding Principles

01. **Reflexive Making** — Constant questioning of the tool and the result.
02. **Frugal Innovation** — Maximising utility with minimal resources.
03. **Salvage-Design** — Repair, reuse, and repurpose over buying new.
04. **Inclusive Co-Creation** — Making with communities, not for them.
05. **Open Source as Empowerment** — Technical transparency as a social right.

Mushroom cultivation monitor built with Cris Silva, Pramo, and Brian at Dinacon 2022. Grown, not bought.

### [Doodle3D SG](https://github.com/saadcaffeine/Doodle3D-Transform-SG)
*Developer · Tech Salvage*
Revives an orphaned open-source project — adapting Doodle3D Transform to run offline or in remote environments via Raspberry Pi, making 2D-to-3D drawing accessible without reliable internet.

### BrailleRap SG
*Maker · Inclusive Design / Tech Salvage*
Open-source braille embossing printer built from a repurposed 3D printer, 3D-printed parts, stepper motors and a solenoid. Replicated from Fab23 Bhutan through Singapore — Singapore's first makerspace-assembled BrailleRap — and the Philippines, with work toward Cameroon, Thailand and Cambodia. Building manual, firmware, STL files and translation software are shared openly.

### FoldedFlex & DBDC — next braille concepts
*Maker · Inclusive Design*
Two low-cost refreshable braille concepts following BrailleRap, listed here as concepts, not finished devices:

- **[FoldedFlex](https://github.com/saadcaffeine/foldedflex)** — v0.1 test-strip design for a folded flex-PCB coil actuator. Design-rule checked, but not yet fabricated, folded or tested.
- **[DBDC](https://github.com/saadcaffeine/dbdc)** — v0.2 prototype design for a 3D-printable display using one printed cam disc and one small DC motor per cell, instead of six piezo actuators. Fully modelled and print-ready, but not yet built or tested.
