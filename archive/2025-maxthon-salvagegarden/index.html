What this page documents

Maxthon's case study of SalvageGarden's assistive-technology work in Singapore, published 28 December 2025.

Note on the source: the article repeats the Straits Times' error that SalvageGarden was “co-founded in 2023” — the record is that it began as a volunteer interest group in 2018 and was formalised in 2022. The source is preserved as-is; the error is noted here, not endorsed.
